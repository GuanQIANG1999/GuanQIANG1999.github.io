LLM Inference at the Wireless Edge

First-author research · Western University · 2026

LLM inference at the wireless edge has to work within computing and communication constraints. My research examines where model computation should run and how limited resources should be allocated.

My contribution

  • First-author research on value-aware resource allocation and layer partitioning for LLM inference.
  • Studies resource assignment and the division of model execution between devices and the wireless edge as connected decisions.

Engineering relevance. Addresses the system decisions behind serving a model: execution placement, resource use, and the constraints of the deployment setting.

Focus: LLM inference, edge computing, model partitioning, resource optimization.
