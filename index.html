Efficient and Secure Distributed Learning

First-author research · Western University · 2023 – 2025

Distributed learning requires coordinating model execution across participating devices. My work examines both the allocation of limited resources and the integrity of clients in collaborative training.

My contribution

  • First-author work on jointly selecting client-side DNN partition points and server CPU shares, using optimization for profiled systems and a modified TD3 policy for uncertain systems (IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2025).
  • First-author work combining packet-level risk ranking, RFE feature selection, TVAE minority-class augmentation, and risk-adapted Hashcash admission checks (IEEE PIMRC, 2023).

Engineering relevance. Connects model partitioning and resource management with the reliability of participants in distributed AI systems.
