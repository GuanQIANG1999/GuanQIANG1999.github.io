01 / HEALTHCARE AI2026 — Present

Turning complex clinical data
into a working ML pipeline.

An end-to-end system for healthcare data analysis, built to handle incomplete evidence and imbalanced clinical data.

THE ENGINEERING VALUE

Connect data preparation, multimodal features, prediction, and evaluation in one pipeline that runs in a secure HPC environment.

  • Applied ML
  • Multimodal data
  • HPC
Explore the project +

The challenge

Clinical records can be incomplete and class distributions uneven. A useful analysis pipeline needs to address these conditions throughout preprocessing, modeling, and evaluation.

My contribution

  • Designed and implemented the end-to-end AI/ML pipeline for a Mitacs research project.
  • Connected healthcare data preprocessing, multimodal feature extraction, clinical risk prediction, and model evaluation.
  • Developed models for incomplete and imbalanced data and deployed the pipeline in a secure high-performance computing environment.

Related research

CRS-Triage studies confidence- and reliability-aware selective triage under incomplete clinical evidence. Manuscript under review for AAAI 2027.

02 / EFFICIENT INFERENCE2026
DEVICE
⇄
WIRELESS EDGE

Making LLM inference fit
the wireless edge.

Research on value-aware resource allocation and layer partitioning for LLM inference with limited computing and communication resources.

WHY IT MATTERS

Inference design has to account for where computation runs and how limited resources are assigned.

  • LLM inference
  • Edge AI
  • Optimization
Explore the research +

The question

How should model layers and resources be allocated when LLM inference spans devices and a wireless edge?

My focus

First-author research on joint resource allocation and layer partitioning, with a value-aware perspective on serving inference workloads.

Research status

“Value-Aware Resource Allocation and Layer Partitioning for LLM Inference at the Wireless Edge” is under review at IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (2026).
